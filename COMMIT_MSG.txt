feat(card-scripts): destroy all [type] sweeper targets (#2594 follow-up)

The drafter has 3 FDN cards wanting this — Day of Judgment
("Destroy all creatures."), Fumigate ("Destroy all creatures.
You gain 2 life for each creature destroyed this way."), and
Ultima ("Destroy all artifacts."). The current `Destroy` op
only supports single targets via `REMOVAL_TARGETS`; sweepers
needed a separate "all <type>" enum value. This lane adds
`REMOVAL_ALL_TARGETS` (`"all_creatures"`, `"all_artifacts"`,
`"all_enchantments"`, `"all_nonland_permanents"`) and threads
the engine's `applyEffect` path to iterate the battlefield and
call `destroyCard` for each matching permanent.

## Schema
- `target-filters.ts`: new `REMOVAL_ALL_TARGETS` constant and
  `RemovalAllTarget` type. `RemovalFilter.target` is widened to
  accept the union of single-target and sweeper values.
- `schema.ts`: `removalFields.target` is a union of the two
  enums. Both `DestroySchema` and `ExileSchema` (the lane only
  threads Destroy; Exile keeps single-target support for now)
  accept the wider enum.
- `matchesRemovalFilter`: the existing switch in
  `target-filters.ts` is extended with the four sweeper cases.

## Engine
- `interpret.ts` `case "Destroy"`: when `effect.target` is in
  `REMOVAL_ALL_TARGETS`, the engine iterates over the
  battlefield(s) narrowed by `effect.controller` (default: all
  players), filters by `matchesRemovalFilter`, and calls
  `destroyCard` on each match. `destroyCard` already handles
  indestructible + regenerate shields; `min_power` / `max_power`
  still apply for "all_creatures with power X+" sweepers.
- `script-guards.ts` `isTargetedEffect`: the `Destroy` case
  returns false for sweeper targets so `effectTargetCount`
  reads as 0 — the dispatcher loop doesn't try to consume a
  non-existent target slot from the spell's `targets` list.

## Sample cards
- `cards/day_of_judgment.json`: "Destroy all creatures."
- `cards/fumigate.json`: "Destroy all creatures. You gain 2
  life for each creature destroyed this way."
- `cards/ultima.json`: "Destroy all artifacts."

## Tests
6 new in `destroy-all.test.ts`:
- Day of Judgment card-script declares `all_creatures`
- Fumigate card-script declares `all_creatures` + GainLife
- Ultima card-script declares `all_artifacts`
- end-to-end: Day of Judgment's `all_creatures` sweep moves
  every creature on every battlefield to its owner's graveyard
  while leaving pure artifacts (Mind Stone) in place
- end-to-end: Ultima's `all_artifacts` sweep destroys artifacts
  and artifact creatures, but leaves pure creatures in place
- end-to-end: Fumigate's `all_creatures` + GainLife chain
  resolves both effects
