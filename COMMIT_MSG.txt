feat(card-scripts): counter target [color] spell (#2594 follow-up)

The drafter has 1 FDN card wanting this — Flashfreeze (FDN #590,
"{1}{U}, Instant, Counter target red or green spell."). The
script-side `Counter` op accepted any spell on the stack; this
lane extends `CounterSchema` with an optional `colors: ["R",
"G"]` array and threads the engine's `effectTargetLegal` to
filter the targeted spell by the source card's color identity
(#2594 follow-up).

## Schema
- `CounterSchema` gains `colors: z.array(z.enum(["W","U","B","R",
"G"])).min(1).optional()`. Default `undefined` preserves the
existing counter-anything behavior.

## Engine
- `interpret.ts` `effectTargetLegal`: when `op: "Counter"` and
`colors` is set, the targeted stack object's source card's
`color_identity` must contain at least one of the listed colors.
A colorless source card (artifact spell, colorless card) is
rejected.

## Sample card
- `cards/flashfreeze.json`: "Counter target red or green spell."

## Tests
5 new in `counter-color.test.ts`:
- Flashfreeze script fixture declares `colors: ["R", "G"]`
- schema accepts Counter with colors: [W] (smoke shape)
- end-to-end: a Flashfreeze-style script with `colors: ["R",
"G"]` counters a red spell's source card on the stack; the
source card lands in its owner's graveyard (CR 701.5b)
- end-to-end: a colorless source card is rejected — the spell
stays on the stack
- regression: Counter with no `colors` filter still counters
any spell (existing behavior preserved)
