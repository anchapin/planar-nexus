## Summary

Lane 74 of the Wave 4.8 follow-up 6 loop (#2614). The
schema (`StaticSchema.protectionFrom: string[]`) and
engine (`hasProtectionFrom` in evergreen-keywords.ts)
already support multi-quality protection: the per-instance
ledger is data-driven (`perInstance.some((q) => q ===
color)`). Lane 74 is mostly a test-suite addition that
verifies the multi-color / multi-quality path through the
`refreshScriptedStatics` → `hasProtectionFrom` pipeline.

## Pattern

- Akoumep Halberd ("Enchanted creature has protection from
  red and from blue")
- Niveous Wisps ("Enchanted creature has protection from
  red and from everything")
- The "creatures you control have protection from [color A]
  and from [color B]" family

## What this lane adds

A new dedicated test
`lane74-multi-quality-protection.test.ts` (4 cases):

- Schema parses `protectionFrom: ["red", "blue"]` (Akoumep
  Halberd pattern).
- Schema parses `protectionFrom: ["red", "everything"]`
  (Niveous Wisps pattern).
- After `refreshScriptedStatics`, the target's
  `protectionFrom` ledger carries the multi-quality array,
  and `hasProtectionFrom` correctly returns `true` for both
  qualities and `false` for unrelated qualities.
- A creature the anthem does not affect (opponent's
  creature) is NOT stamped with the protection (the
  `affects.controller: "you"` filter still applies).

## Engine-size budget

No change. No schema or engine changes — multi-quality
protection was already data-driven via the lane 71
`protectionFrom: string[]` substrate.
